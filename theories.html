Teaching Learning Approach and Learning Theories

This video explains the importance of active learning in a 4IR/5IR classroom, supported by effective learning theories.

Theories Supporting Active Learning:

  • Constructivism: Learners build their own understanding through experience.
  • Experiential Learning: Learning through doing and reflecting.
  • Social Learning: Learning from observation and interaction.
