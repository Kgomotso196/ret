EDTC112 Preamble by Ms Bogosi, R. L.

EDTC112 is a module that prepares future educators to integrate technology into the classroom. It introduces us to tools, theories, and strategies that support 21st-century teaching and learning. Through hands-on projects involving coding, robotics, and digital media, we learn to create engaging, tech-enhanced learning environments for our students.
